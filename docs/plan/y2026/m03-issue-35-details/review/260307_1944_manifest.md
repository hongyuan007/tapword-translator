### Task Objectives
This change **attempted to** resolve scroll-drift of the floating translation tooltip on pages where `<body>` acts as an independent scroll container (e.g., `developers.openai.com/codex` with `position:relative` + `overflow-y:auto` on `<body>`). On such pages `window.scrollY` remains `0` while `document.body.scrollTop` accumulates, causing the tooltip's computed `top` CSS value to decrease on every scroll step and the tooltip to visually drift upward away from its anchor word.
