**Task Objectives**:
This change set attempted to fix four interconnected bugs in TapWord Translator v0.4.2:

1. **Suppress regression**: With `suppressNativeLanguage=ON`, English words on Chinese pages were not being suppressed. Root cause: block context with mixed CJK+Latin returned "auto" from language detection.
2. **Single-click not respecting setting**: `validateSingleClickSelection` was hardcoded to always suppress, ignoring the `suppressNativeLanguage` toggle.
3. **zh→en same-language fallback broken**: Pure Chinese text (e.g., "不整虚拟机") translated to Chinese instead of English because `resolveTargetLanguage` received "auto" from block context.
4. **Japanese Kanji misidentified as Chinese**: Pure Kanji words on Japanese pages (no Kana) were indistinguishable from Chinese by Unicode regex, causing spurious zh→en fallback.
